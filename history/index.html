THE HISTORY
OF ALLIUM.

Allium has survived vanilla updates, world resets, ownership changes, experimental half-seasons, Skyblock, Create, trains, wars, factories and airships. This is the surviving chronicle of the server: reconstructed from announcement archives and screenshots preserved by the community.

SEASONS 1–2
2019 — 2020

The beginning

Allium was founded June 6, 2019. The earliest surviving material shows a small community server that rapidly developed the traits that would define it for years: shared projects, a public-facing community, server tours, recruitment and an emphasis on building together rather than simply occupying the same map.

By early 2020, Allium was publicly describing itself as a friendly community server. The second season reached as many as 18 players online at once—a meaningful milestone for the young server. Patreon support and a website appeared during this era, while planning for Minecraft's Nether Update forced the first serious conversations about how Allium would handle major version changes and resets.

The 1.16 transition ultimately became the dividing line between the early Allium worlds and the next era.

SEASON 3
Aug. 16, 2020 — 2021

The community formula takes shape

Season 3 launched on August 16, 2020 in the 1.16 era. Recruitment posts went live immediately, protected islands were reserved from private bases, and the first Dragon Fight was scheduled for August 30. Towns and communal infrastructure increasingly became part of the server's identity.

In March 2021, Devoid left for Navy boot camp and placed the server in the staff team's hands, noting that Allium was approaching its second birthday. The world remained on 1.16 while he was away. When 1.17 arrived, the plan initially was to update without a reset and expand the border for new terrain.

SEASON 3.5 / 4
Jun. — Dec. 2021 onward

A false start, then the real Season 4

A 1.17 Season 4 was announced for June 2021 with a temporary 2,000×2,000 border, continuing towns, a revamped Discord and restored website. By August 26, that world was formally reclassified as Season 3.5: it was not meeting expectations, and a proper rerelease would wait for Caves & Cliffs Part II.

The proper Season 4 launched December 4, 2021 at 11:00 AM on Minecraft 1.18. Players granted themselves permissions through Discord, the End was locked for at least two weeks, and the season deliberately emphasized large community farms over redundant lag-heavy private farms. The community also elected mayors, held a Nether Hub design competition, organized Secret Santa, added a new admin, built a formal path network, and fought the Ender Dragon on December 30.

Season 4 also continued Allium's habit of treating infrastructure as a shared aesthetic project: roads had standards, bridges and tunnels were expected to look intentional, and even intersections were signed.

SEASON 5
Sep. 3, 2022 — Mar. 2023

A mature vanilla SMP

Season 5 was announced in late August 2022 and ultimately launched early on September 3, 2022, with a voice-chat release party and Donor/Donor+ giveaways. By this point the seasonal rhythm was established: seed selection, launch party, controlled progression, community infrastructure, markets and a world download at the end.

The world became another large communal build canvas, with the server's increasingly elaborate End rooms and transport projects carrying forward into later seasons.

SEASON 6
Mar. 17, 2023 — Oct. 2023

Towns, borders and deliberate progression

Season 6 launched in March 2023 around towns, a deliberately small starting border, markets and delayed access to the End. The restrictions were meant to concentrate players and make transportation and settlement matter. Elytra convenience was explicitly questioned in favor of rail lines, decorated Nether tunnels and other infrastructure.

On June 9, 2023, terrain beyond 3,000 blocks was reset and regenerated for Minecraft 1.20, bringing armor trims, archaeology and cherry groves into the existing season instead of immediately discarding the world.

SEASON 7
Oct. 27, 2023 — Jun. 2024

The long season

Season 7 opened on October 27, 2023 at 5:30 PM on Minecraft 1.20.2. Towns were again a major focus, and Simple Voice Chat was encouraged to make the increasingly social server feel more immediate.

Season 7 stretched long enough to develop an intermission of its own. On May 7, 2024, Allium launched Season 7.5 Skyblock with another pre-release party and giveaway. In late April the community was already voting on whether Minecraft 1.21 should extend Season 7 or begin a new world. The new-season option won out, setting up Season 8.

SEASON 8
Jun. 21, 2024 — 2025

Five years of Allium

Allium celebrated its fifth birthday in June 2024. The Season 8 roadmap tracked the Minecraft 1.21 server ecosystem as Spigot, Paper, Pufferfish and Purpur builds became available and the plugin stack was tested. A community poll selected the launch time.

Season 8 opened June 21, 2024 at noon, preceded by the now-traditional voice-chat party and Donor+ giveaway. The launch screenshots preserve one of the clearest records of how large launch days had become.

SEASON 9
Apr. 26, 2025 — Jun. 2025

Allium discovers Create

After a break, the announcement on April 18, 2025 was simple: “ALLIUM IS COMING BACK.” Players voted between vanilla, lightly modded and a heavily modded Create-centered season. A public development stream followed on April 23, and by April 24 the in-development server was already open for experimentation.

The first Create season launched April 26, 2025 at noon. New players got a Create basics stream before launch, and Prism Launcher became the supported route for installing the much larger modpack. This was a major break from Allium's semi-vanilla roots: machines, factories, railways and engineering were no longer side projects—they became the world.

SEASON 10
Jun. 21 — Oct. 28, 2025

Back to vanilla — but bigger

Season 10 returned to vanilla Minecraft 1.21.5 and launched June 21, 2025 at noon. The map began constrained and expanded in stages; the first expansion opened a 3,000×3,000 area with new biomes and structures, while the Nether was deliberately held back until later.

The season quickly developed two major towns, Nexus and Regalia. Their rivalry culminated in a server-wide battle on June 29, 2025: unaffiliated players were invited to pick a side, and Regalia won. At the same time, the world was being tied together by a formal railway initiative. Double-track routes, controlled grades, bridges, tunnels and visual standards were mandated, turning transport into another communal build project.

Season 10 ended on October 28 as the server shut down in preparation for another Create season. Players were warned to save schematics and world downloads before access closed.

SEASON 11
Nov. 8, 2025 — 2026

The second Create season

Season 11 launched November 8, 2025 at 2:00 PM. The pack now included systems such as Origins alongside Create, and the server leaned even harder into infrastructure and an industrial economy.

By November 17 the market was open with Netherite Ingots as currency. Suggested products included bulk metals, AE2 hardware, mining contraptions, backpacks, magic equipment and airplanes. The Black Line train service began at the same time, linking Market, GeorgeWashinalot, the planned MinecraftYeti stop, Nexus, Hazelgrove and Gurimina's Castle. Players were explicitly encouraged to ride the train to reduce server-side lag.

SEASON 12
May 8, 2026 — 2026

Create: Aeronautics

Season 12 was announced on April 26, 2026 under a name that summarized the next escalation: Create Aeronautics. The seed and modpack were released ahead of time for base hunting, a dedicated trailer arrived May 6, and the server opened on May 8, 2026 at roughly 4:10 PM.

Airships and aircraft changed the visual language of the server. Where previous worlds had roads and trains, Season 12 could put entire moving constructions into the sky.

Season 12 airshow: airships, rotorcraft and airborne machinerySeason 12 • exact capture time unavailable

An impromptu airshow was announced for July 18 with prizes in three categories, though that particular scheduled event was cancelled shortly before it began. By August 2026 the Discord was joking about rewarding Allium's 1,000th member with a $10 Chili's gift card—an absurdly Allium milestone for a server that had begun seven years earlier as a small SMP.

SEASON 13
2026 — PRESENT

The current era

Season 13 continues the heavily customized 1.21.1 NeoForge direction, with Create engineering, deep-ocean exploration, custom structures, Ars Nouveau, vehicles, automation and a much larger curated modpack. Distribution moved toward a Prism-based setup with automated pack updating, allowing Allium to ship a cohesive instance even when individual mods could not simply be redistributed through a conventional public modpack.

The server now carries seven years of accumulated habits: launch parties, shared markets, named towns, giant public infrastructure, ridiculous engineering projects, community events and a willingness to completely reinvent the rules of the next season.
